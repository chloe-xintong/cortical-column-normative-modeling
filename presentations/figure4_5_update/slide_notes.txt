Slide 17: Figure 4B: acquisition and sampling explain part of local similarity
Generate independent Gaussian noise on each native DWI grid, sample at real O3 coordinates with trilinear interpolation, then recolor to match subject-specific depth mean and 21×21 covariance. FWHM=0 means no additional Gaussian PSF, not an absence of interpolation-induced smoothness. Local excess L(d)=D_far−D(d), far reference 20–30 mm. Residual=L_real−L_null. AUC is not a proportion of biological information. Nonzero PSF sensitivity from older analyses is not merged into this formal primary result.

Slide 19: Figure 4D: no consistent positive DK-boundary shape effect
ΔD=(1−r_across)−(1−r_within). Aggregate matched-pair mean → anchor-bin mean → bilateral subject-region-bin mean → equal-region subject-bin mean → group median/IQR. Effect is conditional on boundary-eligible anchors and distance matching. Curve uses varying eligible anchors across bins. It is not directly comparable to A’s all-anchor within-region medians. Earlier positive-boundary conclusions are superseded. A small negative aggregate does not establish that DK anatomy is invalid or that cross-region profiles are intrinsically more alike.

Slide 20: Figure 5 methods: a controlled representation comparison
Outer 5-fold CV, inner 5-fold CV, stratified by age quantile bins. Median imputation and StandardScaler fit within each training fold. Tune Ridge alpha over logspace(−4,6,21) using inner MAE; evaluate held-out outer folds. Metrics are recomputed from merged OOF predictions. Subject-bootstrap intervals condition on the saved OOF predictions; they do not include model retraining or resampling of the full CV pipeline. Age is from the matched clinical visit. Use fixed LSQR outputs consistently.

Data provenance: Sources: run_ridge.py; saved fold definitions; submission_lsqr_20261028.json.
