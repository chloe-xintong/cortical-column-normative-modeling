# Figure 6 notebooks

Only two formal 186-participant notebooks are retained:

1. `figure6_DK_region_model_selection_186.ipynb` contains the DK-region model-selection and
   Gaussian-versus-warped calibration results used to freeze the primary model.
2. `figure6_ico4_final_model_results_186.ipynb` contains final ico4 OOF calibration,
   full-cohort model validation, original-scale conditional centile curves, and Figure 6C
   age-effect preparation. Its final section also displays the integrated four-panel overview
   and the complete numerical summary.

The subsequently frozen Figure 6C standardized estimand, support audit, tables, surface data, and
maps are stored reproducibly under `../ico4_warped_m2_186/figure6c/`; they do not alter the frozen
model-selection or calibration notebook results.

The old 188-participant pilot notebook, four model-specific notebooks, and the superseded
187-participant intermediate notebook/results were removed to avoid ambiguity.
